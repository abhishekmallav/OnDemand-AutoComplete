Usage Guide

Quick reference on how ondemand autocomplete works.

OnDemand AutoComplete

A fast, local, privacy-respecting text expansion extension. All your snippets and settings are stored locally on your machine.

Alt + Shift + . (period) Open the in-page Spotlight search to insert any snippet directly into the current field.
Tab / Space / Enter Trigger expansion when configured in Trigger Activation Key settings.

Insert these tags into your snippets to automatically populate live data when expanding:

{{date}} Current formatted date
{{time}} Current time
{{datetime}} Combined date & time
{{clipboard}} Paste current contents of clipboard
{{cursor}} Places typing caret here after expansion
{{day}} Current day of the week (e.g. Monday)
{{month}} Current month (e.g. October)
{{year}} Current four-digit year

New Snippet
